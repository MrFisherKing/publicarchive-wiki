About Me

Hey there! My name's Trent. I've been an amateur Ham Radio operator (callsign WA9RAN) since 1978. I'm also obsessed with tracking our lovely Earth's geomagnetic activity. It all started when I witnessed an overwhelming aurora borealis in Chapleau, Ontario when I was a boy and my fathers Ham radio was rendered useless long distance for several days. I have my own amateur weather station and I monitor the ionosphere so you don't have to!

I update weekly. Please head down and sign the guestbook below.

Geomagnetic Data NEW!

I log all my own geomagnetic data alongside NOAA K-Index bulletins. Normally my data-sets match 1:1 with minimal variation. However the second week of February 1995 was anything but usual.

February 1995 Readings

From February 5th to Feb 12th my Fluxgate Compass (DIY rig) recorded off the chart activity despite official NOAA records leveling around K-1 and K-2. Admittedly, this is DIY rig so inaccurate recordings are entirely possible. I do take great care in my equipment so I'm confident I have something here. Any other locals have data to share?

FIG 1. Fluxgate log for Feb 5th - Feb 12th. Blue line is NOAA official records. Green line is my deviated readings. Peak deviation was Feb 7th, dipping up and down between K-6 and K-9 until the 10th and then steadily descending back to baseline by the 12th.
