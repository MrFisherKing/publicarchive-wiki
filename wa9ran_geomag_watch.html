About Me

Hello! My name is Trent and I've been a licensed amateur radio operator (callsign WA9RAN, Extra class) since 1979. I live in rural Marytown and I run this page as a hobby to track solar and geomagnetic activity, since it has a big effect on HF radio propagation — if you're a fellow ham you know exactly what I mean!! I also dabble in home weather monitoring with a Davis Instruments weather station on my roof.

This page gets updated whenever I have new data worth sharing. Feel free to sign my guestbook below.

Geomagnetic Data NEW!

Since last spring I've been logging my own local magnetometer readings alongside the official K-index bulletins from Boulder, just as a hobby project. Most days it's boring and matches the official numbers just fine. The first week of February was NOT one of those weeks.

February 1995 Readings

Starting the night of Feb 2nd my homemade fluxgate rig started showing a sustained deviation that did not match ANY of the official K-index bulletins for that period — Boulder's numbers stayed quiet (K=2, K=1) the whole week, real calm stretch. My own readings, though, showed a spike that doesn't correspond to anything in the public record. I want to be careful here and not jump to conclusions — my equipment is homemade, and it's entirely possible I've got a calibration problem or some local interference (there's a substation about 2 miles from my QTH). But I've never seen a deviation like this before or since, and I thought it was worth sharing in case anyone else logging geomag data locally saw something similar that week.
